Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this book only for people currently in a narcissistic relationship?

No. It's written for anyone who wants to understand these patterns — whether you're in a difficult relationship, healing from one, or simply want to recognize the warning signs before they show up.

Can I read it if I'm dating and not married?

Yes. Understanding these patterns early, while dating, is one of the most valuable times to learn them. The book also covers what healthy love and a healthy marriage look like, for whenever you get there.

What if I'm not sure whether my partner is narcissistic?

That's exactly what this book helps with. It doesn't ask you to label anyone. It teaches you to recognize patterns clearly, so you can understand what you're dealing with and decide what to do next. Only a qualified professional can diagnose Narcissistic Personality Disorder.

Does the book discuss marriage and healthy relationships?

Yes. The final part of the book is about rebuilding yourself, recognizing healthy love, choosing a healthier partner, and building a healthy relationship and marriage.

Is this a physical book or a digital ebook?

It's a digital ebook, purchased and delivered through Selar. No physical copy is shipped.
